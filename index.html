FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does an AI project cost?

It depends on the features, data sources and integrations involved. Share your requirements through the form and I will send you a clear quote.

Can you build the whole product, not just the AI?

Yes. I work across the AI workflow, backend, front-end and UI, so the AI capability ships as a complete product.

Can an AI assistant answer from my own documents?

Yes. That is what a RAG system is for: it retrieves relevant passages from your content and uses them to ground the answer.

Do I need a multi-agent system?

Not always. A single agent is simpler and often enough. Multiple agents suit work that splits into distinct specialist steps, and I will recommend the simpler option when it fits.

Which technologies do you use?

Mainly Python, LangGraph, LangChain, FastAPI and SQL, chosen to suit the project.

Can you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I can work with clients worldwide over email and video calls.
